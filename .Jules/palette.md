## 2024-07-25 - Required Field Indicators

**Learning:** Adding explicit required field indicators (like a red asterisk `*`) and an explanatory note to forms is a simple, low-cost way to significantly improve usability. It prevents user frustration by clarifying expectations upfront, reducing validation errors and making the interface more intuitive.

**Action:** For any future forms I encounter in this application, I will ensure that all required fields are clearly marked. This small detail is a fundamental aspect of good form design and accessibility.
