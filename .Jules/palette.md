## 2025-05-22 - Improving Form Accessibility and Delight in Streamlit
**Learning:** Adding `help` tooltips and required field indicators in Streamlit forms significantly improves accessibility by providing both visual and screen-reader context. Emojis in select boxes add a layer of visual delight that makes the app feel more personal and engaging.
**Action:** Always include `help` parameters and clear required indicators (`*`) in form inputs to reduce cognitive load and improve accessibility.
